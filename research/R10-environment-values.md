# R10: Environment values verification and Remote Earth profile

## Short answer
- **ISS values mostly confirmed (one qualifier gap):** `< 1 s` TDRS delay (R5 #1) and `1.5–36 h` evacuation / `≤ 24 h` medical-care-facility requirement (R5 #15) are **confirmed**. Flight Surgeons working MCC console `"Surgeon"` and weekly PMCs are **confirmed** (R5 #13), while `"24/7"` is **not found** in R5 #13's cited sources (24/7 MCC flight-controller staffing is confirmed separately on NASA's JSC MCC page).
- **Moon values confirmed (with precision notes):** Operational lunar delay `4–12 s` one-way (R5 #4), far-side and South Pole visibility gaps (R5 #5), `72–144 h` return window (R5 #15), and `1/6 g` gravity (R5 #16) are **confirmed**. Note that `~0.166 g` comes from NASA's *Moon Fact Sheet* (`1.62 m/s²`, `0.165–0.166` of Earth) rather than the *Moon Facts* page.
- **Mars values confirmed:** No evacuation capability (R5 #15), `~12 h/day` without direct Earth line-of-sight from the surface (R5 #9), `0.38 g` gravity (R5 #16), and `24 h 39 m` / `24.6 h` sol (R5 #18a; `24.6597 h` solar day vs `24.6229 h` sidereal in NASA's *Mars Fact Sheet*) are **confirmed**.
- **Care lead (Levels of Care & EIMO) confirmed:** Historical NASA-STD-3001 Levels I–V, Level V `"autonomous"` care with a physician CMO for Mars `> 210 d` (R5 #12), and Rev C's shift to PRA tailoring are **confirmed**, as is the verbatim EIMO definition (R5 #10).
- **Remote Earth profile — Concordia Station (Dome C, Antarctica):** Used by NASA (ESA-NASA *CHOICE* study) and ESA (`"White Mars"`) as a spaceflight analog. In winter (~9 months, Feb–Nov), evacuation is **impossible**, external communication is **satellite phone and satellite internet only** (constrained telecom), and on-site care is led by the **station physician** in Concordia's hospital alongside an **ESA research medical doctor** (24/7 SAR lead) backed by remote telemedicine.

## What this means for the app (proposal)
*All items below are proposals for the team, not decided product copy.*

- **Safe environment-switch values ready for `src/core/` / `content/`:**
  - **ISS:** `< 1 s` TDRS delay; near-continuous link; evacuation `1.5–36 h` (`≤ 24 h` medical requirement); `~0 g`; `24 h` clock; Ground Flight Surgeon (`"Surgeon"`) directs care.
  - **Moon:** `~1.3 s` one-way light delay (`4–12 s` operational lag cited as research gap); far-side / South Pole direct-to-Earth gaps; return `72–144 h` (3–6 days); `1/6 g` (`~0.166 g`); hybrid crew/ground care.
  - **Mars transit (default):** `3–22 min` one-way delay + `~2–3 week` conjunction blackout every `~26 months`; no evacuation; `~0 g` transit; Level V autonomous care led by onboard physician CMO with async Earth telemedicine.
  - **Mars surface:** `3–22 min` one-way delay + `~12 h/day` surface loss of Earth line-of-sight + `~2–3 week` conjunction blackout; no evacuation; `0.38 g` gravity; `24 h 39 m` (`24.6 h` rounded) sol; Level V autonomous care by onboard CMO/physician crew.
  - **Remote Earth (Concordia Station winter-over):** Near-real-time satellite phone/internet when linked (constrained telecom); no evacuation for `~9 months` (~Feb–Nov); `1 g` (3,233 m hypobaric hypoxia); `24 h` clock (4 months polar night); on-site station doctor + ESA MD with home telemedicine.
- **Proposed escalation wording when an action card says `"act"` (≤ 12 words each, grounded in verified sources):**
  - **ISS (proposal, 9 words):** `Contact the Flight Surgeon ("Surgeon") in Mission Control now.`
  - **Moon (proposal, 10 words):** `Call the Flight Surgeon if linked; act now if unlinked.`
  - **Mars transit (proposal, 10 words):** `Act with onboard physician CMO now; message Flight Surgeon asynchronously.`
  - **Mars surface (proposal, 11 words):** `Act with surface CMO now; queue message for next Earth relay.`
  - **Remote Earth — Concordia winter (proposal, 10 words):** `Alert the station doctor now; consult home specialists via satellite.`

## Not verified / open questions
- **`"24/7"` Flight Surgeon console staffing in R5 #13 sources:** None of R5 #13's three cited sources explicitly state `"24/7"` for Flight Surgeons; *JSC Mission Control Center* confirms MCC is staffed `24/7` with flight controllers, and *Flight Surgeons* confirms Flight Surgeons work the `"Surgeon"` console during missions. If UI copy says `"24/7"`, cite *JSC Mission Control Center* for MCC 24/7 staffing rather than attributing `"24/7"` directly to the *Flight Surgeons* page.
- **Exact Concordia winter satellite bandwidth & blackout frequency:** Opened ESA primary pages state `"Satellite phones and internet are the only means of communication"` and `"limited in our telecom means"`, but do not state exact kbps/Mbps caps or outage hours/day (and an Italian OGS technical link `ricerca.ogs.it` returned HTTP 403). Keep Remote Earth's comms note qualitative (`"Satellite phone & internet only · limited bandwidth"`).
- **Exact operational Moon FOD latency vs research gap:** R5 #4 calls `4–12 s` `"operational lunar communication delays"` modeled by NASA; Slide 21 of `20250000703` specifically lists `"Lunar communication delay (4-12 seconds one-way)"` under `Key Research Gaps`, while the 2 coded lunar studies tested `< 15 s` (`2.5 s` in Hurst et al. 2015).
- **Hailey TM cross-reference typo:** `NASA/TM-2017-219290` quotes `"NASA-STD-3001, Vol 1"` for Levels I–V text (pp. 6–10) while its Table 1 caption writes `"NASA-STD-3001, V2, Rev A, Table 13"` (p. 5); Rev C removes static Levels-of-Care tables in favor of PRA tailoring (`§1.2.1`).
