# Brief R4: first-person emergency mode

First read `research/briefs/00-RULES.md` and follow it.
**Your output file:** `research/R4-emergencies.md`. Write nothing else.

## Goal

Choose which emergencies Sara can handle **by herself**, offline, with one-tap step-by-step guidance. The app is for the person using it, not for helping a crewmate.

## Questions

1. **What happens in flight.** Which medical conditions are most likely on long missions? Use the NASA Integrated Medical Model (IMM), ISS medical event reports and the HRP medical risk. Rank them by how often they happen and how severe they are.
2. **Candidates.** For each, can a person manage it alone, and what are the first steps? Then name the red flags and the point where it needs another person or Earth.
   - chest pain
   - dizziness or fainting
   - severe headache
   - eye injury or sudden vision change
   - burn
   - bleeding cut
   - allergic reaction
   - smoke or toxic air
   - kidney stone pain
   Use first-aid and medical sources (Red Cross, AHA, NASA). Write steps as short screen instructions.
3. **Recommendation.** Which 3–5 emergencies should the MVP include, and why?
4. **Safety wording.** How do medical apps word "this is not a diagnosis", and when to escalate? Look for good examples.
