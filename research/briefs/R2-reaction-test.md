# Brief R2: reaction test ("tap when green")

First read `research/briefs/00-RULES.md` and follow it.
**Your output file:** `research/R2-reaction-test.md`. Write nothing else.

## Goal

Give us the science to build a short daily reaction check-in. It should tell Sara when she is slower than her own normal.

## Questions

1. **What NASA uses.** What reaction tests do astronauts use? Cover the Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT and the 3-minute PVT-B) and the NASA "Cognition" battery (Basner et al.). How long does each take, and what does it measure?
2. **Metrics.** How are these defined?
   - mean or median reaction time
   - lapses: the threshold in ms for PVT-B vs the 10-minute PVT
   - false starts
   - reciprocal reaction time (1/RT)
3. **Normal values.** Typical values for healthy adults and for astronauts or analog crews (ISS, HERA, Antarctica). Give the spread from day to day.
4. **What makes it worse.** How do sleep loss, fatigue, workload and spaceflight change results? Give effect sizes.
5. **Personal baseline.** How do studies set one person's normal: how many sessions, practice effects, and when a change counts as meaningful?
6. **Browser test.** Known issues with running a reaction test on a web page or tablet (input lag, timing accuracy), and how to handle them.
