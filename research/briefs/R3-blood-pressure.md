# Brief R3: guided blood pressure measurement

First read `research/briefs/00-RULES.md` and follow it.
**Your output file:** `research/R3-blood-pressure.md`. Write nothing else.

## Goal

Give us correct, sourced steps so Sara can measure her own blood pressure alone, plus what the numbers mean in space.

## Questions

1. **Self-measurement steps.** The step-by-step method from current guidelines (AHA, ESC, or similar). Cover:
   - rest time
   - posture and arm position
   - cuff size
   - number of readings and the gap between them
   - what to avoid beforehand
   Write each step so it can go on a screen as one short instruction.
2. **Categories.** Current blood pressure categories (normal → crisis), with the source and year.
3. **Red flags.** Which readings or symptoms need urgent action? Give the exact thresholds.
4. **In space.**
   - How does microgravity change blood pressure and heart rate (fluid shift, in-flight vs pre-flight values)?
   - What do long-duration crews show around months 3–6?
   - Does anything about measuring in microgravity change the steps (posture, arm at heart level)?
5. **NASA practice.** How do ISS crews measure blood pressure in flight (device, schedule)?
