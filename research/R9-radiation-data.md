# R9: Radiation Data and Exposure Limits

## Short answer
- **MSL/RAD cruise data (`MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0`):** Hosted on NASA PDS PPI as `218` daily ASCII `.TXT` files (`~5.54 GB`) plus `.LBL` labels (`~131 MB`) spanning `2011-12-06` (`C010`) to `2012-07-14` (`C231`). RDR files report absorbed dose rate in **`microGray / Hour`** from silicon Detector B and tissue-equivalent plastic scintillator Detector E — **no Sv column is shipped**; the RAD team derived `1.84 ± 0.33 mSv/day` GCR (`0.48 ± 0.08 mGy/day`) using ICRP-60 `Q(L)` (`<Q> = 3.82 ± 0.25`). SPDF `obs-l2` CDFs omit the `2011-340`–`2012-196` cruise series.
- **MSL/RAD Mars surface data:** Lives in the same PDS bundle (`5,351` surface rows, `~6.6 MB`/sol typical) and on NASA SPDF `obs-l2` (`~64–77 KB`/day CDFs). Published surface GCR averages (first ~300 sols, solar max) are **`0.64 mSv/day`** (`0.21 ± 0.04 mGy/day`, `<Q> = 3.05 ± 0.30`), while NASA and SwRI press releases report **`0.67 mSv/day`** total average (`0.65 mSv/day` when scaled to Dec 2022 solar phase in *Nature* 2024).
- **Solar particle events (SPEs):** Yes — the PDS cruise files include **5 SEP events** summing to **`24.7 mSv`** (`19.5 mSv` on Mar 7–15, `4.0 mSv` on Jan 23–29, `1.2 mSv` on May 17, 2012), adding **`~5%`** to the `≈ 466 mSv` total cruise dose equivalent. On Mars's surface (Aug 2012–June 2013), NASA reports no major solar storms and `> 95%` of total dose from GCRs.
- **Other environments (dose equivalent per day):**
  - **ISS (LEO):** NASA `80 mSv` (solar max) to `160 mSv` (solar min) per 6-month stay (`~0.44–0.88 mSv/day`); active DOSTEL measurements `0.731 mSv/day` (2019) and `0.68 mSv/day` (Dec 2022).
  - **Lunar surface (Chang'e-4 LND):** `1.369 mSv/day` (`1,369 μSv/day`, `<Q> = 4.3 ± 0.7`, Jan–Feb 2019 solar minimum, no SPEs); `0.81 mSv/day` scaled to Dec 2022.
  - **Lunar transit / orbit (Artemis I Orion):** `0.96–1.24 mSv/day` GCR inside Orion (`26.7–35.4 mSv` over 25 days, Dec 2022, ICRP-60 `<Q>` = `2.30–3.06`); low lunar orbit `1.55 mSv/day` (LRO/CRaTER scaled to Dec 2022).
- **Career exposure limit (`NASA-STD-3001 V1 [4030]`):** Section `4.8.2` caps a crewmember's `"total career effective radiation dose"` at `"less than 600 mSv"`, explicitly `"universal for all ages and sexes"` (computed with NASA `Q` and 35-year-old female reference phantom parameters).
- **App data recommendation (proposal):** Because raw PDS cruise files total `~5.54 GB` with absorbed dose only (`µGy/h`) and SPDF `obs-l2` excludes cruise, no compact public daily cruise `mSv/day` file exists to drop straight into `data/nasa/rad/`; ship the verified published daily rates (`mSv/day`) and `NASA-STD-3001` limit (`600 mSv`) as constant baselines.

## What this means for the app (proposal)
- **Do NOT download PDS RDR cruise files (`~5.54 GB`) or `obs-l2` CDFs (`~64–77 KB`/day, surface-only, no `Sv`) into `data/nasa/rad/` for runtime conversion:** PDS RDR lacks `Sv` and requires integrating LET histograms with `Q(L)`, silicon-to-water factors, and dead-time/geometry unfolding, while SPDF `obs-l2` omits the cruise phase entirely.
- **Ship published NASA/RAD daily averages cleanly as verified constant baselines** (matching the brief's fallback path), storing provenance in `src/core/constants.ts` and `data/nasa/rad/README.md`:
  - **Career ceiling (`NASA-STD-3001 V1 [4030]`):** `600 mSv` (`< 600 mSv`, all ages/sexes); habitat design targets `[V1 4033]`: `1.3 mSv/day` free space, `0.9 mSv/day` planetary surface; acute SPE ceiling `[V1 4031]`: `250 mSv`.
  - **Environment default rates (`mSv/day`, GCR nominal unless noted):**
    - **ISS (LEO):** `0.731 mSv/day` (`DOSIS 3D` 2019 solar min, including SAA) or `0.68 mSv/day` (Dec 2022), within NASA's `0.44–0.88 mSv/day` (`80–160 mSv` / 6 months) solar-cycle band.
    - **Lunar orbit / cislunar transit (Artemis I Orion):** `0.96–1.24 mSv/day` (inside Orion, Dec 2022; midpoint `1.10 mSv/day`; unshielded/lightly shielded low lunar orbit `1.55 mSv/day`).
    - **Lunar surface (Chang'e-4 LND):** `1.369 mSv/day` (solar minimum `~1 g/cm²`) / `0.81 mSv/day` (Dec 2022 solar phase).
    - **Mars transit (MSL/RAD):** `1.84 mSv/day` GCR (`± 0.33`, Zeitlin et al. 2013), plus optional SEP event spikes (`+24.7 mSv` across 5 events / `+19.5 mSv` for a strong pair like March 2012, `~5%` of total in weak Solar Cycle 24 rising phase).
    - **Mars surface (MSL/RAD):** `0.64 mSv/day` GCR (`0.67 mSv/day` NASA/SwRI reported 10-month average; `0.65 mSv/day` Dec 2022).
- **Optional raw NASA file if a local sample is still desired:** Fetch one small ISS TEPC CSV slice from NASA CDAWeb HAPI (`ISS_DOSANL_TEPC`, which directly ships `DOSE_EQ60` in `uSv/min`), rather than attempting to reconstruct `mSv` from raw MSL/RAD RDR `.TXT` files.

## Not verified / open questions
- **Direct primary PDF check of Zeitlin et al. (2013) and Hassler et al. (2014) on `science.org`:** Both *Science* papers are behind a publisher paywall (`10.1126/science.1235989`, `10.1126/science.1244797`), so Table 1 and exact page numbers in the *Science* print versions were not inspected directly. Their cruise and surface figures (`1.84 ± 0.33 mSv/day`, `0.48 ± 0.08 mGy/day`, `<Q> = 3.82 ± 0.25`, `24.7 mSv` / `19.5 mSv` SEPs, `<Q> = 3.05 ± 0.30`, `0.64 mSv/day`, `0.21 ± 0.04 mGy/day`) were verified through open-access papers by the RAD authors (`Guo et al. 2015 arXiv:1503.06631v1`, `Zeitlin & La Tessa 2016 PMC4810318`) plus `PMC5771487` and `PMC11976309`; specifically, the exact source reconciliation explaining NASA/SwRI's **`0.67 mSv/day`** vs peer-reviewed **`0.64 mSv/day`** (and the surface-only SPE contribution in `mSv` over the 300-sol period beyond NASA's `"more than 95 percent"` GCR statement) could not be read verbatim inside Hassler et al. (2014).
- **Uncertainty on cruise `<Q>` (`± 0.25` vs `± 0.30`):** Both Zeitlin & La Tessa (2016, `PMC4810318`) and Guo et al. (2015, `arXiv:1503.06631v1`) give **`3.82 ± 0.25`**; secondary summaries occasionally cite `± 0.30` (likely rounding/confusing with `± 0.30` surface `<Q>` or `1.75 ± 0.30 mSv/day`), which could not be checked against the paywalled Zeitlin et al. (2013) *Science* Table.
- **Exact Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.20536564` filenames and byte sizes:** Direct HTTP requests to Zenodo returned `403 Forbidden`, so individual CSV filenames/byte sizes on Zenodo remain unverified (only DataCite metadata and column/cadence description were retrieved).
