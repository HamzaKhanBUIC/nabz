# R11: Link each action card to NASA's hazards and HRP risks

## Short answer

- **Five Hazards of Human Spaceflight:** NASA's Human Research Program (HRP) defines five interacting hazards—Space Radiation, Isolation and Confinement, Distance from Earth, Gravity Fields, and Hostile/Closed Environments—on its Hazards Overview (`https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/`) and individual hazard pages.
- **HRP risks 99, 100, 179, 95, and 175 link to specific hazards on HRR and HSRB DAGs:** NASA's Human System Risk Board (HSRB) Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) map **179 (Cardiovascular)** and **95 (Medical Conditions)** to all 5 hazards; **100 (Sleep)** has all 5 hazards as exposure nodes (driven mainly by Isolation/Confinement, Hostile/Closed Environments, and Altered Gravity); **99 (Behavioral Health)** models 3 Hazard nodes in its DAG (Isolation and Confinement, Distance from Earth, Radiation) while its HRR Risk Statement also cites Altered Gravity; and **175 (EIHSO)** models 1 Hazard node (Distance from Earth).
- **Space Radiation HRP risk IDs:** The active standalone radiation risk is **`i=96` (Risk of Radiation Carcinogenesis)**; three historical radiation risks were merged in July 2020 (IRP Revision L) into active multi-hazard risks—**`i=102`** (CNS effects) into **`i=99`**, **`i=98`** (cardiovascular/degenerative tissue effects) into **`i=179`**, and **`i=97`** (acute SPE syndromes) into **`i=95`**.
- **Proposed card-to-risk-to-hazard mapping:** Below is a **proposal** mapping all 17 card templates in `content/cards.json` (plus the planned radiation dose card) to HRP risk IDs and NASA hazards, with every link citing the NASA HRR/HSRB page that justifies it.

## What this means for the app (proposal)

*This section is a **proposal** for linking every card in `content/cards.json` (plus the planned radiation dose card) to HRP risk IDs and NASA's 5 Hazards, grounded in the NASA pages above.*

- **Tagging convention (proposal):** Keep card badges concise by showing the primary HRP risk ID(s) directly tied to the card's measurement/action, and list the primary hazard(s) from that risk's HSRB DAG / HRR Risk Statement (with secondary hazards optional in a "more details" view so every card isn't overloaded with all 5 badges).
- **`checkin-retake` ("Check-in could not be scored") — proposal:** Link to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** (`BHP Monitoring Capability`: regular valid cognitive assessments; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/behavioral-health-risk-dag-narrative-approved-sa-06808.pdf p. 2) and **`i=175` (EIHSO)** (`Monitoring Capability` / `Data Accessibility` / `Procedure Design` for crew-administered self-testing; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=175 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/eihso-risk-dag-narrative.pdf p. 2). **Hazards:** **Distance from Earth** (autonomous data quality without ground operator oversight; `i=175` DAG) and **Hostile/Closed Environments** (card advises finding a quiet spot; noise/habitability factors on https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-hostile-closed-environments/).
- **`baseline-building` ("Learning your personal normal") — proposal:** Link to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** (`BHP Monitoring Capability` establishing individual cognitive baselines; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/behavioral-health-risk-dag-narrative-approved-sa-06808.pdf p. 2), with secondary **`i=100` (Sleep)** (daily alertness tracking; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100). **Hazards:** **Isolation and Confinement**, **Distance from Earth**, **Space Radiation** (`i=99` DAG; `i=99` Risk Statement also notes **Gravity Fields**).
- **`all-normal` ("Within your personal normal") — proposal:** Link to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** (`Cognitive Function` and `BHP Monitoring Capability`; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/behavioral-health-risk-dag-narrative-approved-sa-06808.pdf pp. 1–2). **Hazards:** **Isolation and Confinement**, **Distance from Earth**, **Space Radiation** (`i=99` DAG; plus **Gravity Fields** in `i=99` Risk Statement).
- **`reaction-watch` ("Slightly below your personal normal") — proposal:** Link to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** (HRR Mitigation Strategy highlights early detection of cognitive changes from baseline; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99) and **`i=100` (Sleep)** (card recommends pacing work, short breaks, and full sleep; `Sleep Risk` links workload/fatigue to cognitive readiness on https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/sleep-risk-dag-narrative-sa-07566.pdf p. 1). **Hazards:** **Isolation and Confinement**, **Distance from Earth**, **Space Radiation**, plus **Hostile/Closed Environments** and **Gravity Fields** (via `i=100` DAG & `i=99` Risk Statement).
- **`reaction-slow` ("Slower than your personal normal") — proposal:** Link to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** (`Cognitive Function` -> `Crew Capability` -> `Task Performance`; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/behavioral-health-risk-dag-narrative-approved-sa-06808.pdf p. 1) and **`i=175` (EIHSO)** (card defers precision tasks and asks a crewmate to double-check critical procedures, addressing `Individual Readiness` -> `Task Performance` under Earth-independent operations on https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=175 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/eihso-risk-dag-narrative.pdf pp. 1–2). **Hazards:** **Isolation and Confinement**, **Distance from Earth**, **Space Radiation** (`i=99` & `i=175` DAGs; plus **Gravity Fields** in `i=99` Risk Statement).
- **`reaction-slow-short-sleep` ("Slower than your normal after short sleep") — proposal:** Link primarily to **`i=100` (Sleep)** (`Sleep Deficiencies` -> `Fatigue` -> `Individual Readiness` / `Behavioral (Risk)` on https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=100 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/sleep-risk-dag-narrative-sa-07566.pdf p. 1), plus **`i=99` (Behavioral Health)** and **`i=175` (EIHSO)** (deferring precision tasks / crewmate double-check; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=175). **Hazards:** **Isolation and Confinement**, **Hostile/Closed Environments**, **Gravity Fields** (the three main drivers named in `i=100` DAG Narrative p. 1), plus **Distance from Earth** and **Space Radiation** (`i=100` DAG exposure nodes & `i=99` DAG).

- **Planned radiation dose card — proposal:** Link primarily to **`i=96` (Risk of Radiation Carcinogenesis)** (tracks `Estimated Mission Radiation Dose` + `Prior Mission Exposures` -> `Estimated Career Dose` against agency standards; https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=96 and https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/radiation-carcinogenesis-risk-dag-narrative.pdf p. 2), with secondary links to **`i=99` (Behavioral Health, absorbing `i=102` CNS effects)**, **`i=179` (Cardiovascular, absorbing `i=98` degenerative tissue effects)**, and **`i=95` (Medical Conditions, absorbing `i=97` acute SPE syndromes)**. **Hazards:** **Space Radiation** (primary; `i=96`, `i=99`, `i=179`, `i=95` DAGs) and **Distance from Earth** (`i=96` DAG: outside Earth's magnetosphere / vehicle shielding constraints).

## Not verified / open questions

- **No official NASA card-to-risk mapping exists:** NASA's HRR and HSRB publish hazard-to-risk DAGs and narratives, not mappings for our app's 17 UI card templates; the card-level mapping in "What this means for the app (proposal)" is a grounded proposal rather than a NASA specification.
- **Minor discrepancy on Risk `i=99` (Altered Gravity):** The HRR `i=99` Risk Statement (`https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=99`, section "Risk Statement") explicitly names "altered gravity" alongside isolation/confinement, distance from Earth, and radiation, whereas the newer HSRB Behavioral Health DAG (`behavioral-health-dag-code-sa-07566.txt`) includes only 3 `"Hazard"` nodes (`Distance from Earth`, `Isolation and Confinement`, `Radiation`) and routes gravity-related inputs through `Other Risks` (`Sensorimotor`, `SANS`).
- **Retired radiation risk IDs (`i=97`, `i=98`, `i=102`):** These IDs no longer appear on the active HRR `/risks/` index (`https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/risks/`); each URL now shows a July 2020 *IRP Revision L* merge notice pointing to `i=95`, `i=179`, and `i=99` respectively. The app should cite active ID `i=96` (plus `i=99`/`i=179`/`i=95`) and only mention `i=97`/`i=98`/`i=102` as historical IDs if needed.
- **Non-Ionizing Radiation Risk (`NIR`):** Listed on HHP (`https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/non-ionizing-radiation-risk/`) for UV/laser ocular and skin injury without an active `i=` risk page on HRR `/risks/`; not applicable to the ionizing radiation dose card, though relevant if eye/skin laser hazards are ever added.
