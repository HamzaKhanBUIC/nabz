# R1: NASA data sources

## Short answer

- **Challenge Resources tab (`spaceappschallenge.org`):** Lists 5 reference links (3 NASA HRP conceptual pages: *5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight*, *Risks of Human Spaceflight*, *Ongoing HRP Research in Space*; plus 2 Canadian Space Agency articles on space radiation monitoring and ISS science). None are downloadable raw time-series datasets, so the app must pair them with NASA standards, HRP evidence, and OSDR data.
- **NASA HRP Hazards & Risks:** Our three Must features map directly to 4 primary Human Research Roadmap (HRR) risks across all 5 NASA hazards (*Space Radiation*, *Isolation and Confinement*, *Distance from Earth*, *Gravity Fields*, *Hostile/Closed Environments*): Cognitive/Behavioral Risk (`i=99`) and Sleep Loss/Circadian Risk (`i=100`) for the reaction check-in; Cardiovascular Adaptations Risk (`i=179`) for blood pressure; and Inflight Medical Conditions Risk (`i=95`) plus Earth-Independent Operations Risk (`i=175`) for offline first-person emergency mode.
- **NASA OSDR (`osdr.nasa.gov`):** Hosts open-access Inspiration4 human clinical/biomarker CSV datasets (`OSD-569` CBC, `OSD-575` CMP & cardiovascular/immune cytokines, `OSD-656` urine inflammation) and Axiom-2/JAXA/bed-rest omics (`OSD-903`, `OSD-943`, `OSD-530`, `OSD-353`, `OSD-195`). However, **none of the open OSDR datasets have per-day in-flight physiology (HR, BP, sleep, PVT)**: they use discrete pre/post-flight ground timepoints (`L-92` to `R+194`), while NASA Twins Study and Inspiration4 wearable/Cognition datasets sit behind controlled access (LSDA / TRISH EXPAND).
- **NASA-STD-3001 (Vol 1 Rev C & Vol 2 Rev F) + referenced `OCHMO-STD-100.1A`:** Provides exact requirement IDs for cognitive monitoring (`[V1 4011]`), sleep/circadian scheduling (`[V1 4014]`, `[V1 6001]` 8.5 hr sleep, `[V2 6079]` NC-40 noise, `[V2 8055]` lighting), medical autonomy & non-physician usability (`[V1 3004]`, `[V2 7043]`, `[V2 7045]`), private store-and-forward comms (`[V1 6002]`–`[V1 6003]`), and workload (`[V2 5007]` Bedford ≤ 3). Resting BP (`140/90 mmHg` hypertension) and HR extreme limits (`>130 bpm` / `<30 bpm` awake) come from `OCHMO-STD-100.1A` (mandated via `[V1 3001]`), while `NASA-STD-3001` itself sets quantitative aerobic limits (`[V1 4001]`–`[V1 4003]` VO2max) and IMM medical condition likelihoods (Appendix E).
- **Core recommendation (3 NASA pillars):** Anchor the app on **(1) NASA-STD-3001 + `OCHMO-STD-100.1A`** for limits, usability rules, and Appendix E event probabilities; **(2) NASA HRP Risks (`i=99, 100, 179, 95, 175`)** for Day-120 drift narratives and action-card reasoning; and **(3) NASA OSDR (`OSD-569`, `OSD-575`)** for real open-access astronaut lab CSV baselines (`L-` vs `R+1`).

## What this means for the app (proposal)

These are **proposals** for how to ground our simulated astronaut data and Must features in NASA sources rather than inventing arbitrary numbers:

1. **Core Source 1 — `NASA-STD-3001` (Vol 1 Rev C & Vol 2 Rev F) + `OCHMO-STD-100.1A` (Standards, thresholds, UI rules, and medical event list):**
   - **Simulated ranges & alert ceilings:** Anchor Sara's sleep goal at **8.5 hr/day** (`[V1 6001]`) with a daily 1–7 subjective sleep quality check (`OCHMO [6039]`), set clinical hypertension caution/escalation bounds at **140/90 mmHg** (`OCHMO Sec G.3`) and extreme resting HR bounds at **<30 bpm (awake) / >130 bpm sustained** (`OCHMO Sec G.8`), and store her personal pre-flight baseline across simulated `L-90 / L-45 / L-3` sessions (`OCHMO Table 8`).
   - **Events to inject:** Draw emergency-mode scenarios and day-120 anomalies directly from **Appendix E (Table E.2-1 & Table E.2-2)** — e.g., *Late Insomnia / circadian misalignment*, *CO2-related headache*, *orthostatic intolerance*, *anaphylaxis / acute cardiac event* — and enforce `[V2 7045]` (non-physician first-person usability) and `[V2 5007]` (low cognitive workload, Bedford ≤ 3) in the step-by-step emergency UI.
   - **Privacy & comm delay:** Model `[V1 6002]`–`[V1 6003]` (private medical data, store-and-forward under 40-min Mars delay) as the rationale for keeping check-ins local/private unless Sara chooses to transmit.

2. **Core Source 2 — NASA HRP Risk Evidence (`HRR i=99, i=100, i=179, i=95, i=175`) (Day-120 mission drift & Action Card explanations):**
   - **Drift over the mission (Day 1 → Day 120):** Model realistic long-duration transit drift rather than flat noise: mild cumulative sleep debt & circadian drift (`i=100`), subtle reaction-time slowing and variance growth (`i=99`, PVT/Cognition), and microgravity cardiovascular deconditioning / altered resting HR & orthostatic response (`i=179`, `i=180`).
   - **Action Card ("what changed / why it matters / what to do"):** Link each flagged deviation directly to its named HRP risk and hazard (e.g., citing *Sleep Loss & Circadian Desynchronization Risk* and *Isolation & Confinement* / *Hostile Closed Environments* when reaction time slows after short sleep, or *Cardiovascular Adaptations Risk* during fluid-shift/BP checks) and cite *Earth Independent Operations (`i=175`)* for why local action replaces waiting 40 minutes for Earth.

3. **Core Source 3 — NASA OSDR (`OSD-569` CBC & `OSD-575` CMP/Cardiovascular Cytokines) (Real open-access astronaut lab baselines):**
   - **Real NASA data integration:** Ingest the actual open-access Inspiration4 CSVs (`LSDS-7_Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_TRANSFORMED.csv` from `OSD-569` and `LSDS-8_Comprehensive_Metabolic_Panel_CMP_TRANSFORMED.csv` + `cardiovascular_EvePanel` from `OSD-575`) to seed realistic pre-flight (`L-92`, `L-44`, `L-3`) vs post-flight (`R+1`, `R+45`, `R+82`) biomarker reference bands and delta magnitudes for crew profiles or lab-context panels.
   - **Honest gap handling:** Because OSDR open datasets do not contain per-day in-flight HR, BP, sleep, or PVT time series, explicitly label our daily time series (Days 1–120) as **synthetic longitudinal telemetry calibrated to NASA-STD-3001 / OCHMO-STD-100.1A limits, HRP drift mechanisms, and published PVT/cardiovascular flight distributions** (to be specified quantitatively in R2, R3, and R6).

## Not verified / open questions

- **Per-day Inspiration4 wearables & `Cognition` raw values:** We confirmed `OSD-569` through `OSD-575`, `OSD-630`, `OSD-656`, and `OSD-687` on OSDR do not include Apple Watch HR/SpO2/ECG or iPad `Cognition` reaction-time CSVs. Those multimodal clinical/wearable datasets appear to live under TRISH EXPAND / SOMA controlled access rather than open OSDR downloads, so exact per-day Inspiration4 wearable distributions were not directly inspected.
- **NASA Twins Study raw physiology files:** Confirmed absent from open OSDR search results and governed by LSDA controlled access (`lsda.jsc.nasa.gov`); we did not submit a data request or inspect individual Twins Study per-day vitals/cognitive records. (Note: an external search result mentioned dbGaP `phs001453`, but fetching that dbGaP page did not verify NASA/Twins Study content, so it is excluded.)
- **Exact quantitative PVT reaction-time thresholds & microgravity BP cuff adjustments:** Neither `NASA-STD-3001` nor `OCHMO-STD-100.1A` specifies a numeric millisecond cutoff for PVT reaction-time slowing or microgravity arterial blood-pressure shifts; those numbers require dedicated literature research (briefs R2 and R3).
- **Exact `NASA-STD-3001` Volume 1 Rev B vs Rev C "Levels of Care" numbering:** In Rev C, in-mission medical care is framed around PRA / Integrated Medical Model (`[V1 3004]`, Appendix E) rather than a numbered "Level of Care I–V" table; if the team wants explicit Mars Level of Care V checklists, historical Rev B or Exploration Medical Capability (ExMC) references should be checked separately.
